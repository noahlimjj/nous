Common questions

How do I add the home screen widget?
Touch and hold an empty area of your home screen, tap the + button, search for Nous, then choose the small or medium widget. It shows today's habits and focus time.

How do I turn on daily reminders?
Open Settings in the app, turn on "daily reminder" and pick a time. Allow notifications when iOS asks.

Can I use Nous without an account?
Yes. Choose "continue as guest". Guest data stays with that guest session, so sign up with email if you want to keep it across devices.

How do I delete my account?
Open Settings, scroll to actions and tap "delete account". This permanently removes your account and all of your habits, sessions and goals.
