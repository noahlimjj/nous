Privacy Policy

Nous — Study Habit Tracker

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Nous ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in the Nous iOS application and web service.

1. Overview & Principles

Nous is built to empower focused study and mindful daily habits. We believe your study data belongs to you. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers.

2. Information Collected

  • Account Credentials: If you sign up with an email and password or Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication processes and stores your authentication credentials securely. If you use Guest Mode, your sessions are stored locally or anonymously without personal identification.
  • Study & Habit Data: Habits you create, study timer session durations, milestones, and daily check-ins are saved to your secure cloud database (Google Cloud Firestore) so you can synchronize across devices.
  • Mood Ratings: Optional daily mood and focus reflections you choose to record.

3. How Information Is Used

Your data is strictly used to provide the app's functionality:

  • Tracking habit streaks and timer logs.
  • Generating personal milestone progress and weekly focus summaries.
  • Syncing your study statistics across your authorized devices.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data transmitted between the app and our backend uses industry-standard TLS encryption. Cloud data is hosted within Google Firebase infrastructure with strict Firestore Security Rules restricting data access solely to your authenticated account ID.

5. Third-Party Services

Nous relies on trusted cloud infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication and Firestore database hosting (Firebase Privacy Policy).

6. Account Deletion & Data Rights

You have full control over your data. You can delete individual habits, timer sessions, and logs directly in the app settings at any time. To permanently delete your account and all associated data, open Settings in the app and tap "delete account", or contact us at the email below.
